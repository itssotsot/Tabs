## 0.0.3

- On Windows, videos no longer flicker.

## 0.0.2

- **Split view: two tabs side by side.** Right-click a tab and choose **Open in Split View**, or drag a tab to one side of the page. The split button in the toolbar swaps the sides or closes it.
- A split shows as one row in the tab list, with both of its tabs, and moves as one when you drag it.
- In a Google Meet call, the call's tab shows the faces of the people in it. Hover over them for their names.

## 0.0.1

The first version of Tabs, a browser that's better with friends.

- **Send any page to a friend or a group in one keystroke** (**⌘⇧S**, or the **Send** button). Videos start at the second you were watching, and links leave out tracking tags.
- Chat with friends and groups in the sidebar. Every friend gets a chat, and groups are one invite or join code away.
- Two tab layouts: a vertical sidebar, or tab groups per chat. Tabs from the same site group together in the site's own color.
- Bring over bookmarks, history and open tabs from your other browsers in the welcome tour, or later from File › Import Bookmarks and History.
- Pick a theme (Default or Paper) and an accent color. The toolbar takes on the color of the page you're on.
- Play/pause and progress right in the tab list, for videos and music on YouTube, X, Instagram, Reddit, Spotify and more.
- **Mute yourself in a call from any tab.** A call's tab has mic, camera and sound buttons in the tab list, and Google Meet shows you muted.
- **Sites ask for your camera, microphone and more right under the address bar.** Allow every visit, allow this time, or don't allow, and see yourself or your mic level before you choose. Settings › Site access has the rest.
- A tab using your camera, microphone or screen shows it, in the tab list and the address bar, where you can stop it.
- Built-in ad and tracker blocking, including YouTube's video ads.
- Chrome extensions from the Chrome Web Store, with toolbar buttons, pop-ups, shortcuts and side panels.
- Netflix, Spotify and other streaming sites play, and Memory Saver rests tabs you're not using.
- **Updates install themselves.** When one is ready, click **Update** and Tabs restarts on the new version, or it installs the next time you quit. What changed shows up right here after each update, and under Help › What's New.
